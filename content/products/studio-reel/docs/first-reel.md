## Check the kit works

> Render the example reel.

This renders the bundled example to `out/three-checks.mp4`.

## Make your own

> Make a 30-second reel about 3 mistakes people make when they start running.

Claude writes the script, records the voiceover, paints the animation, adds captions, music and sound effects. Your
reel is in `out/`: a vertical MP4 (1080×1920) with a cover image next to it.

## Change something

> The first 3 seconds are too slow. Make the hook hit harder and render again.
