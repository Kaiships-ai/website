How it works

One prompt.
Six jobs. One MP4.

You type what the reel is about. Claude runs a crew of six steps, one after another, and hands you a 1080×1920 file ready to post.

  1. 01 · WRITER

    It writes the words.

    Hook first, one idea per reel, in plain spoken lines. Claude scores the hook and only ships it at 8/10 or better.

  2. 02 · NARRATOR

    A voiceover, free.

    Natural text-to-speech in one take. No microphone, no voice subscription, no API key.

  3. 03 · PAINTER

    Every scene, painted.

    Hand-drawn characters and props, drawn stroke by stroke in code. Each chapter is one continuous camera move.

  4. 04 · CAPTIONS

    Word by word, on the beat.

    Captions locked to the spoken words, inside Instagram's safe zone.

  5. 05 · COMPOSER

    Music and sound effects.

    Generated on your machine and timed to the words.

  6. 06 · REVIEWER

    It checks its own work.

    Claude scans the whole MP4, fixes what is wrong and logs every fix in REVIEW.md, up to 3 rounds. Then you get the reel.
